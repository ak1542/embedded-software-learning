bit manipulation
set_field(reg, value, mask)
Replace only the bits selected by mask; keep every other bit of reg.
(reg & ~mask) | (value & mask)
reg
10110110
mask (amber = the field)
00111100
value
00101000
↓ step 1: reg & ~mask clears the field
reg & ~mask
10000010
↓ step 2: value & mask keeps only the field bits of the new value
value & mask
00101000
↓ step 3: OR them together
result
10101010
Bits outside the mask are unchanged. Without value & mask, a value with extra bits set would corrupt neighbouring fields.
